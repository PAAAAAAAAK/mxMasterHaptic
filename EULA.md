## 2. What the Software does

The Software adds haptic feedback to a Logitech MX Master 4 mouse in response to
mouse input. To do this it observes mouse button, wheel and cursor-movement
events system-wide, using a Windows low-level mouse hook.

The Software:

- observes **mouse events only** and never installs a keyboard hook;
- makes **no network connections** and transmits no data anywhere;
- collects **no personal data**, and no analytics or telemetry;
- stores your preferences locally on your own computer.
